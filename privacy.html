Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Mist International ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or enroll in a workshop, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide when you subscribe to our newsletter, fill out our contact form, or enroll in a workshop — such as your name and email address.
  • Payment information when you purchase a workshop, processed securely through our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.
  • Basic usage data, such as which pages you visit, collected automatically to help us understand how our site is used.

How we use your information

  • To send you the newsletter you subscribed to, and workshop updates you've requested.
  • To process your enrollment and communicate with you about a workshop you've registered for.
  • To respond to messages sent through our contact form.
  • To improve our website and the workshops we offer.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not send marketing emails you haven't subscribed to.

Sharing your information

We share information only with the service providers that help us operate — such as our email delivery and payment processing partners — and only to the extent needed for them to provide that service.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link in any email we send.
  • You can request a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by contacting us at the email below.

Cookies

Our website uses basic cookies to remember your preferences and understand overall site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the site may not work as intended.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, reach us at privacy@mistinternational.example.
